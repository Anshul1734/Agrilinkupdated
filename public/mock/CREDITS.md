# Demo image credits

The photos in `products/` are the lead images of the matching English Wikipedia articles (Tomato, Onion, Potato, ...),
fetched through the Wikipedia API as 600px thumbnails. They come from Wikimedia Commons and are under free licences
(mostly CC BY / CC BY-SA or public domain). Per-image author and licence are on each file's Commons page, linked from the
Wikipedia article. They are demo content only: replace them with your own product photos before a public launch.
